# Privacy Policy — opencheckers

_Last updated: September 16, 2026_

opencheckers ("the app") is a single-player checkers game played against the
computer. This policy explains, plainly, what the app does with your
information: **nothing.**

## Data we collect

**None.** opencheckers does not collect, store, transmit, or share any personal
information or usage data. Specifically:

- The app requests **no Android permissions** and has **no internet access** —
  it cannot send anything off your device.
- There are **no accounts, logins, or sign-ins.**
- There is **no analytics, telemetry, advertising, or third-party SDKs.**
- Nothing is saved: a game in progress and your menu choices live only in memory
  while the app runs.

Because the app cannot access the network, no data ever leaves your device.

## Children

opencheckers is suitable for all ages. Since it collects no data from anyone, it
collects no data from children.

## Third parties

opencheckers contains no third-party services, ad networks, or trackers, and does
not share data with anyone (there is no data to share).

## Changes to this policy

If this policy ever changes, the updated version will be posted at this URL with
a revised "Last updated" date.
